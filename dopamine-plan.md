# 🧠 Dopamine Management Plan
## Integrated with Your 2-Week Health Schedule

This plan overlays your existing health tracker with **dopamine regulation strategies** to improve focus, motivation, and emotional patterns.

---

## 🎯 **Core Principles**

1. **Morning = Low-stimulation window** (6:00-8:00 AM)
   - NO phone/social media until after coffee
   - Natural dopamine from workout + sunlight
   - Sets baseline for the day

2. **Work blocks = Single-task focus** (8:00-12:30, 1:15-5:00)
   - Phone in another room during deep work
   - One browser tab at a time
   - Timer-based focus (25/50 min blocks)

3. **Breaks = Healthy dopamine hits** (9:30, 11:00, 1:15, 3:00)
   - Movement (walk, stretch)
   - Nature/sunlight
   - Music/coffee (NOT scrolling)

4. **Evening = Dopamine wind-down** (7:00-9:30 PM)
   - No screens after 9:00 PM
   - Reading/journaling only
   - Prepare brain for rest

### **🌅 Morning Routine (5:45-8:00 AM)**
| Time | Your Activity | Dopamine Strategy |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| 5:45-6:00 | Wake + Hygiene | **Phone stays off** — Morning cortisol is high, adding stimulation creates anxiety |

**🎯 Goal:** Start the day with EARNED dopamine (workout, food, coffee) instead of CHEAP dopamine (notifications, scrolling).

## 📊 **What to Track in Your Health App**

Add these **optional note tags** to your daily tracker:

### **Dopamine Quality Check**
When you complete an activity, rate it:
- ✅ **Energizing** — Left you focused, calm, motivated
- ⚠️ **Neutral** — Neither good nor bad
- ❌ **Draining** — Left you scattered, anxious, craving more

### **Pattern Triggers**
Note when you feel:
- 🔴 **Procrastination urge** — "I want to scroll instead of work"
- 🟡 **Energy dip** — "I'm tired/unfocused"
- 🟢 **Flow state** — "I'm locked in and productive"

### **Example Notes:**
```
[9:30] Took a 10-min walk outside ✅ Energizing
[11:00] Checked Instagram during break ❌ Draining - wanted to keep scrolling
[2:00] 🔴 Procrastination spike - switched to easier tasks
[3:30] 🟢 Flow state - finished feature in one shot
```

These notes feed into your **3 AM AI analysis** (see below).

## 🤖 **AI Reflection Questions** (Added to Nightly Summary)

Your 3 AM cron job will analyze your day and ask:

1. **What triggered procrastination today?**
   - Look for patterns (time of day, specific tasks, energy dips)

2. **What gave you energy vs. drained you?**
   - Compare ✅ Energizing vs. ❌ Draining activities

3. **What dopamine hits did you choose?**
   - Healthy (walk, coffee, music) vs. Cheap (scroll, notifications, task-switching)

4. **What pattern repeated from yesterday?**
   - Is there a cycle? (e.g., "Always crash at 2 PM", "Always avoid hard tasks in morning")

5. **What would make tomorrow 10% better?**
   - One small tweak (e.g., "Move phone to other room at 8 AM", "Schedule hardest task at 11 AM")

These appear in your **Yesterday tab** every morning.

## 🦊 **Why This Works for Your Brain**

Your brain craves:
- **Novelty** (new info, new stimulation)
- **Rewards** (checking things off, hitting goals)
- **Low friction** (easy dopamine hits like scrolling)

This plan gives you all three, but **redirects them**:
- Novelty → Learning/building (work projects, health progress)
- Rewards → Earned dopamine (workout done, task finished, walk taken)
- Low friction → Structured breaks (timer goes off = guilt-free reward)

You're not REMOVING dopamine. You're UPGRADING the source.
