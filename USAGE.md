## 📱 Daily Workflow

### Morning (5:45 AM)
1. Open tracker on your phone/computer
2. Select today's day (Day 1, Day 2, etc.)
3. Follow the schedule item by item

### Throughout the Day
1. **Check off items** as you complete them
2. **Leave notes** for things that matter:
   - "Oatmeal felt better than banana"
   - "Push-ups easier today, did 12 instead of 10"
   - "Woke groggy, only got 7h sleep"
   - "Skipped this - too busy with work"

### Evening (Before bed)
1. Check your completion percentage
2. Click **"Export Notes for AI Analysis"**
3. Download the JSON file
4. Share it with me (Cléo) in Slack

## 📊 Example Notes (Good vs Bad)

### ✅ Good Notes (Actionable)
```
"Push-ups felt way easier, could do 12-15 now"
→ I increase reps to 12 next week

"Woke at 6:10 instead of 5:45, alarm didn't wake me"
→ I check if sleep quality is dropping

"Greek yogurt parfait super filling, wasn't hungry till 1 PM"
→ I keep this meal, maybe reduce lunch size

"Zone 2 walk at 110bpm perfect, could talk whole time"
→ I know your heart rate target is dialed in
```

### ❌ Less Useful Notes
```
"Done" → I don't know HOW it went
"Good" → What was good? Energy? Taste? Duration?
"Skipped" → Why? Too hard? No time? Not hungry?
```

**Tip:** Brief is fine, but include WHY or HOW something felt.

## 🎯 What to Track in Notes

### Meals

- Taste/enjoyment

- Variations you tried

### Workouts

- How you felt waking up
- Sleep quality (1-10 scale)
- Nighttime bathroom trips
- Dream recall (indicator of REM quality)

### General
- Stress levels
- Work interruptions
- Cravings
- Energy crashes
- Mood

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Page won't load
- GitHub Pages can take 1-2 min after updates
- Use backup URL: https://184-107-106-29.sslip.io/

### Notes not saving
- Check browser isn't in Private/Incognito mode
- Try exporting to confirm data exists

### Schedule looks wrong
- Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
- Clears cached version

### Can't export
- Check browser allows downloads
- Try different browser if blocked
